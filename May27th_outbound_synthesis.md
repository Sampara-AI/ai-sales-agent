# outbound_synthesis_calibration.md

We are no longer fixing infrastructure or workflow.

The remaining issue is:
LLM synthesis quality.

Current outputs feel:
- operationally aware
BUT
- linguistically repetitive
- structurally templated
- commercially generic

The enrichment quality is acceptable.
The synthesis layer is weak.

==================================================
CORE PROBLEM
==================================================

Current emails follow the same hidden structure:

“You likely face X.
vPersonalize solves Y.
What do you optimize for?”

This makes all emails feel similar even when enrichment differs.

We must move from:
category-aware templates

TO:
business-specific commercial observations.

==================================================
REQUIRED FIXES
==================================================

1. STOP generic probabilistic openings

Avoid:
- “You likely face challenges…”
- “Your team may be looking to optimize…”
- “You are probably…”

Instead:
start with:
- specific operational observation
- inferred workflow friction
- scaling implication
- production coordination insight

==================================================
2. PRIORITIZE OBSERVATION → INFERENCE → CONNECTION
==================================================

Correct flow:

1. observe something specific
2. infer commercially plausible friction
3. connect relevant vPersonalize capability
4. explain why it matters operationally
5. provoke curiosity

DO NOT jump immediately to:
“vPersonalize solves…”

==================================================
3. ADD SYNTHESIS DIVERSIFICATION
==================================================

Different companies MUST produce:
- different rhetorical structures
- different reasoning styles
- different operational framing
- different CTA styles

Nike should NOT sound like SquadStudio.
Mizuno should NOT sound like New Balance.

==================================================
4. COMMERCIAL INTELLIGENCE > INFORMATION SUMMARY
==================================================

BAD:
“Mizuno has many products and customization complexity.”

GOOD:
“As customization scales across team sports and golf categories, maintaining consistency between digital configuration, grading workflows, and production turnaround likely becomes operationally expensive.”

We need:
commercial inference.

NOT:
website summarization.

==================================================
5. REMOVE ALL WEAK AI LANGUAGE
==================================================

Avoid:
- likely
- may
- probably
- appears to
- could benefit from

Use:
commercially confident but plausible language.

==================================================
6. CTA CALIBRATION
==================================================

DO NOT ask generic optimization questions repeatedly.

CTAs should feel:
- strategic
- consultative
- operationally aware

Example:
“Curious whether your team currently handles roster-driven customization internally or through downstream production coordination.”

==================================================
7. IMPORTANT
==================================================

Do NOT increase email length.

Target:
120-180 words max.

Enterprise outreach should feel:
sharp
observational
commercially intelligent
low-pressure

==================================================
FINAL OBJECTIVE
==================================================

The recipient should feel:

“These people understand the operational realities behind our business.”

NOT:

“This is another AI-personalized outbound email.”
